About Saki love's to paint

Saki love's to paint is a retro Windows 2000 styled drawing studio featuring dynamic animated strokes.

• Select motion effects like Dots, Trail, Wave, Rainbow, or Pulse to give movement to your drawings.

• Use the Layers panel to create, select, hide, or delete drawing layers.

• Pick a custom brush color and change the desktop or window colors from Options.

• Export your creations using File > Save As... or the Save button.
